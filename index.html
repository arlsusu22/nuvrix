How we work

We review the setup, agree the work, then build and test it with you.

  1. Understand

    We review your goals, current process, tools, and constraints.

  2. Scope

    We agree the deliverables, responsibilities, integrations, and boundaries before implementation.

  3. Build and test

    We configure the system, test the workflows, and review the result against the agreed requirements.

  4. Handover or maintain

    Your team receives documentation and a handover, with ongoing support available where needed.
