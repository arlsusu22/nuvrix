Outbound Stack Set-up

Set up your outbound tools.

We review your sales process, existing tools, and budget, then configure the systems your team needs to research prospects, manage outreach, and track activity.

Implementation project

Who this is for

Companies starting outbound, equipping their first sales team, or replacing a fragmented setup.

Typical scope

We agree the deliverables and boundaries for your implementation before work begins.

  • Existing-tool and sales-process review.
  • Tool selection based on requirements and budget.
  • Prospecting and enrichment tool configuration.
  • Outreach platform configuration.
  • Sending infrastructure and authentication setup.
  • Basic CRM fields, ownership rules, and pipeline configuration needed for outbound.
  • Standard integrations between the selected tools.
  • Basic activity tracking and reporting.
  • Testing, documentation, and team handover.

What you receive

A configured, connected, and tested outbound environment that the team can use.

Engagement
and scope

This service covers the outbound stack and its standard configuration. Complex custom workflows belong in Outbound Automation. Wider CRM migrations and sales-process redesign belong in CRM Implementation & Sales Operations.
