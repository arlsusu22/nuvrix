CRM Implementation & Sales Operations

Implement or reorganise your CRM.

We implement or reorganise your CRM so your team can manage accounts, follow opportunities, and understand what needs attention.

Discuss your CRM↗

Implementation or improvement

Who this is for

Companies introducing a CRM, moving from spreadsheets, or working with inconsistent data and poorly defined sales processes.

Typical scope

We agree the deliverables and boundaries for your implementation before work begins.

  • Sales-process mapping.
  • CRM implementation or reconfiguration.
  • Scoped data migration and cleanup.
  • Account, contact, lead, and deal structures.
  • Pipeline stages and required fields.
  • Ownership and routing rules.
  • Follow-up tasks and sales handovers.
  • Operational reports and dashboards.
  • Relevant integrations.
  • Data validation, documentation, and team training.

What you receive

A usable CRM with clear operating rules, organised information, and reporting aligned with the sales process.

Engagement
and scope

This is broader than the basic CRM configuration included in Outbound Stack Set-up. Migration volume, integration complexity, and process changes are agreed during scoping.
