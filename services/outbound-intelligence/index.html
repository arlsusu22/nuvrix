Outbound Intelligence

Research accounts and monitor your market.

We build intelligence systems around your target market, combining account research, relevant sources, and monitoring rules to deliver useful findings to your sales team.

Discuss your intelligence needs↗

Implementation + optional monitoring

Who this is for

Teams that need better account prioritisation, ongoing market research, or visibility into developments across target companies.

Typical scope

We agree the deliverables and boundaries for your implementation before work begins.

  • Target-account criteria and market definition.
  • Research-source selection.
  • Account identification and qualification.
  • Monitoring of agreed developments, such as new projects, tenders, expansion, hiring, or leadership changes.
  • Account matching and prioritisation.
  • Relevant contact or role identification where information is available.
  • Source links, dates, and an explanation of relevance.
  • Delivery into an agreed destination, such as a CRM, shared sheet, Slack, or email.
  • Optional ongoing monitoring and refinement.

Example scope

Monitoring new project announcements.

An engineering consultancy wants to track newly announced projects in selected markets. The system surfaces relevant announcements, identifies the organisations involved where publicly available, records the reported project stage, and routes the finding to the responsible salesperson.

Illustrative example, not a client case study.

  1. Source

    Public announcement, link, and date

  2. Context

    Organisations and reported project stage

  3. Relevance

    Match against the agreed market criteria

  4. Delivery

    Finding routed to the responsible salesperson

A company event is a reason to investigate. It is not proof of buying intent.

What you receive

An organised research and monitoring process that helps the team decide where to focus its attention.
